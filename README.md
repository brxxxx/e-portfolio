# Teacher E-Portfolio Web Application

A creative, academic-grade **E-Portfolio Website** designed for pre-service and in-service educators, strictly following the **Content Checklist** and aligned with the **Philippine Professional Standards for Teachers (PPST)** and DepEd Domain Framework.

---

## 🎨 Design Philosophy & Color Palette
- **Warm & Lively Brown Theme**: Designed with deep roasted espresso (`#140b05`), dark cocoa leather (`#27170c`), warm hazelnut, and golden amber accents (`#f2a640`). 
- Avoids sterile white backgrounds, replacing them with a cozy, scholarly leather-bound binder aesthetic with glowing radial warmth and cream text (`#fcf4ea`) for readability.
- **Color Vibrancy Switcher**: Click the palette icon in the top navigation to switch between **Rich Mocha**, **Warm Caramel**, and **Deep Espresso**.

---

## 📋 Content Checklist Structure (I - X)
1. **I. Table of Content**: Interactive directory with direct links and a sticky **TS Standards Tracker Bar (TS1 to TS13)**.
2. **II. Introduction**: Statement of purpose, PPST alignment, and formal dedication letter.
3. **III. About the Teacher (Curriculum Vitae)**: Comprehensive bio, educational attainment, teaching competencies, honors, and downloadable CV.
4. **IV. Learning Environment (Partner School)**: San Jose National High School profile, DepEd Vision & Mission plaques, historical milestones, and interactive facility tabs (Quadrangle & Science Lab).
5. **V. Curriculum and Planning**:
   - **Annotated DLL (TS2)** (PDF placeholder & viewer)
   - **Competencies Alignment (TS3)** (PDF placeholder & viewer)
   - **Classroom Management Plan (TS4)** (PDF placeholder & viewer)
   - **Daily Lesson Log (TS5)** (PDF placeholder & viewer)
   - **Lesson Plans (TS6)** (PDF placeholder & viewer)
   - **Instructional Materials (TS6)** (PDF placeholder & viewer)
   - **Assessment Tools and Evaluation (TS7)** (PDF placeholder & viewer)
6. **VI. Professional Development Certificates (All Image)**:
   - **TS9**: Professional Development Organizations (National Congress, Advanced Course, Research Colloquium).
   - **TS10**: LAC Session / School Activities (SLAC Session 4, Reading Month, Mid-Year INSET).
   - Features an interactive **Image Lightbox** with zoom, navigation, and verification metadata.
7. **VII. Microteaching**:
   - **Evaluation Form (TS12)**: Observer scorecard (98.5% Outstanding score).
   - **DLP & Documentation**: Inductive 4A's demonstration highlights and lesson plan.
8. **VIII. Classroom-Based Action Research Proposal (CBAR)**:
   - **Action Research Data Bank (TS1)**: Pre-test vs. post-test diagnostic metrics.
   - **CBAR Manuscript (TS8)**: Project L.I.N.K. full paper abstract and methodology.
   - **Panel Rubric**: Institutional evaluation scores and panel comments.
9. **IX. Teaching Philosophy (TS11)**:
   - 4 Core Pedagogical Pillars (Constructivism, UDL Inclusion, Psychological Safety, Reflective Growth).
   - Interactive Audio Reflection player simulation.
10. **X. Appendices**:
    - **CT Confidential Evaluation (TS13)**: Official sealed envelope notice & transmittal receipt.
    - **Daily Time Record (DTR Form 48)**: 365.5 logged internship hours summary.

## 🚀 How to Run & Customize

### To View in Your Browser:
Simply double-click `index.html` to open it in Chrome, Edge, Brave, or any modern web browser. No server setup required!

### To Customize Teacher Details or Data:
Edit `assets/js/portfolio-data.js` to change:
- Teacher's name, degree, contact details
- Partner school name, vision, mission, and history
- Teaching standards descriptions and scores
- Certificate titles, hours, and issuing bodies

### To Add Your Own Certificates:
Save your certificate image scans (`.jpg` or `.png`) into `assets/images/` and update the image paths in `assets/js/portfolio-data.js`.

### To Preview Your Own PDF Documents:
Click the **"Replace PDF"** button in the PDF viewer toolbar to preview any `.pdf` from your computer live in the modal.
