Introduction

Teaching is an evolving craft that bridges foundational educational theory with the dynamic realities of actual classroom practice. This electronic portfolio serves as a comprehensive culminating record of my Field Study journey as a pre-service teacher, documenting the progressive transition from observer to reflective instructional practitioner. Through direct classroom engagement, faculty mentorship, and immersive school participation, this collection highlights critical competencies in curriculum alignment, instructional design, learner engagement, and classroom management.

The contents of this e-portfolio systematically chronicle the core domains of professional teacher preparation. It outlines the physical and socio-emotional context of the partner school environment, demonstrates structured curriculum execution through Annotated Daily Lesson Logs and detailed lesson plans, and showcases targeted assessment strategies designed to evaluate student learning. Furthermore, it captures active engagement in professional development and Learning Action Cell (LAC) sessions, the practical execution of microteaching demonstrations, and evidence-based inquiry through a Classroom-Based Action Research (CBAR) proposal aimed at addressing authentic classroom challenges.

Above all, this e-portfolio reflects a commitment to lifelong professional learning, reflective teaching, and ethical responsibility in safeguarding student welfare and academic growth. It stands not merely as an archive of completed tasks, but as concrete evidence of a developing pedagogical foundation dedicated to fostering meaningful, responsive, and empowering learning environments.

History of PDMHS

Origin, legislative milestones, and growth of President Diosdado Macapagal High School

President Diosdado Macapagal High School (PDMHS) was established to address the rapid population growth and educational needs of Barangay Katuparan and surrounding areas in Taguig City, originating from an October 1999 community petition led by Virgilio S. Luat and local parents to build a high school on vacant open land fronting the village chapel. Following legislative efforts initiated in the 12th Congress and subsequent land allocation through the Bases Conversion and Development Authority (BCDA), a formal groundbreaking took place on August 15, 2005, through joint support from the Taguig local government under Mayor Sigfrido Tinga and congressional initiative funds. The institution officially opened its doors for classes on August 15, 2007, operating initially as an extension campus named Signal Village National High School - Katuparan Annex with a pioneering corps of 21 teachers under the administration of SVNHS Principal Dr. Domingo G. Manimtim, before celebrating its official inauguration on September 1, 2007.

The school steadily grew into a fully independent public secondary institution, celebrating its first graduation rites for its pioneering four-year high school batch on April 1, 2011, and subsequently receiving major infrastructure developments, including a multi-purpose auditorium and gymnasium built between 2011 and 2012 under Mayor Lani Cayetano. With the national implementation of the K-12 Enhanced Basic Education Program, PDMHS expanded beyond its Junior High School curriculum to offer Senior High School programs, integrating Academic strands and Technical-Vocational-Livelihood (TVL) specializations to serve secondary learners from Katuparan, Signal Village, Pinagsama, and neighboring communities under the Schools Division Office of Taguig City and Pateros.

The Effectiveness of a “Praise and Token” System in Reducing Minor Disruptive Behaviors Among Grade 10 Students in Pres. Diosdado Macapagal High School

Student Researchers John Brix G. Coronejo, Emmanuel P. Ferrer, Aliah T. Wales
Institution & Program TUP Taguig • BTVTED-ICT-CP-4D
Target Class & School Grade 10 TLE - Food Service (Azalea) • PDMHS
Research Facilitator Ms. Annalyn P. Tanto

A classroom-based action research utilizing a One-Group Pre-test–Post-test Design with the Input-Process-Output (IPO) framework. The study introduces an interactive Classroom Currency Economy coupling Behavior-Specific Praise (BSP) with tiered custom animal-themed peso bills (₱10, ₱20, ₱50, ₱100, ₱500, ₱1,000) and the Mystery Pouch Challenge. Systematic observation governed by a strict 5-second operational threshold rule targets minor disruptive behaviors (unnecessary side-talking, off-task inattentiveness, and out-of-seat movement) to reclaim instructional time and foster positive discipline.
